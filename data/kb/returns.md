# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return any product within 30 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

## How to return
Start a return from your account page or ask support to open a return ticket. You will get a prepaid return label by email.

## Refunds
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. Shipping costs are refunded only if the product was faulty.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, opened software and personalised products cannot be returned.
