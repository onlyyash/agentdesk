# Payments

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Klarna, Swish and MobilePay.

## When you are charged
Card payments are reserved when you order and charged when the order ships. Klarna invoices are due 30 days after delivery.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order is put on hold for 48 hours. Update your payment method in your account to release it.
