# Shipping

## Delivery times
Orders inside Sweden and Denmark arrive in 1-3 business days. Orders to the rest of the EU arrive in 3-6 business days. Orders placed before 14:00 CET ship the same business day.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free for orders over 499 SEK. Smaller orders cost 49 SEK for standard delivery and 99 SEK for express delivery.

## Tracking
Every shipped order gets a tracking number by email. The order status in your account changes to "shipped" when the parcel leaves our warehouse.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 5 business days after it shipped, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
